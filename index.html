Typography Vocabulary

Reference these terms while you work. The definitions stay visible so students can learn the vocabulary while diagnosing each example.

Leading
The vertical space between lines of text.
Tracking
The overall spacing added or removed across a range of letters.
Kerning
Fine-tuning the space between specific pairs of letters.
Space Before / After
Space added before or after a paragraph to separate ideas without inserting blank lines.
Alignment
How text sits horizontally inside its text box: left, center, right, or another alignment setting.
Justification
Makes text align to both left and right edges by adjusting word spacing.
Hyphenation
Controls where words can break at the ends of lines with hyphens.
Tabs
Creates predictable columns of text, such as item names and prices, instead of using repeated spaces.
Bulleted Lists
A list structure using bullets with consistent indentation and spacing.
Numbered Lists
A list structure using numbers with consistent alignment and indentation.
Paragraph Styles
A reusable set of paragraph-level formatting such as font, size, leading, alignment, and spacing.
Character Styles
A reusable set of formatting applied to selected characters or words, such as bold, color, or emphasis.
Font Pairing
Choosing typefaces that have complementary roles and create useful contrast without competing.
Line Length
The approximate width of a line of text. Extremely long or short lines can make reading harder.
Widows / Orphans
Controls that help prevent isolated lines of a paragraph from being stranded at the top or bottom of a page or column.
Text-box Alignment
How separate text boxes use coordinated paragraph alignment and text edges to function as one typographic system.

💡 Typography Design Guide

Use these reminders to connect a visual problem to the correct typography control.

Leading
Controls vertical distance between lines.
Tracking
Changes spacing across a range of characters.
Kerning
Adjusts space between specific character pairs.
Paragraph spacing
Space Before/After separates paragraphs without empty lines.
Alignment & Justification
Change how text sits inside its text box. Full justification can create rivers in narrow columns.
Hyphenation
Controls where words can break.
Tabs & Lists
Use predictable columns, indents, bullets, and numbers.
Paragraph styles
Control repeated paragraph-level formatting.
Character styles
Control repeated formatting on selected words or characters.
Font pairing
Choose complementary roles with useful contrast.
